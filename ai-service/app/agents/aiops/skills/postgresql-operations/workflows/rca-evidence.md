# Workflow: RCA evidence package

For every incident, include:
- Incident identifier and observation window.
- Affected cluster/role and application scope (redacted).
- Facts with source, timestamp, and freshness.
- Metrics with units and comparison baseline.
- Interpretation tied to fact IDs.
- Ranked hypotheses with confidence and validation step.
- Unknowns and telemetry gaps.
- Human-review recommendation.
